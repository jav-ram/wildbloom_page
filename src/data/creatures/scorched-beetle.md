A beetle the size of a fist, with legs of grey stone and a body of something like glass that glows bright red, like iron in a forge. It gives off real heat, and when it flies it leaves a trail of embers drifting in the air. They roost in the shrines' old fire basins. A cluster of a few dozen heaped together looks exactly like a roaring bonfire, and travelers have walked up to warm their hands.

The apex flyer of the Pilgrimage: a huge predator built like a kite, with skin stretched taut over a frame of hollow bone and almost no body at all. It hangs motionless far above the light shafts, invisible against the glare, watching the ledges. When it sees prey in the open, it folds its wings and drops. Most delvers never see it until it is already falling on them.

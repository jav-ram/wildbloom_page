A pale, milky eel as long as a rowboat, swimming through the air as if it were water, endlessly circling the light rod. When it strikes a ledge, it bites and coils around its prey, then pulls it off into the open air. Its bite leaves a cold numbness that creeps through the limbs, and victims who survive feel stiff for days.

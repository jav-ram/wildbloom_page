A cloud of moths with ash-grey wings, each the size of a palm. They are drawn to fire the way the pilgrims were drawn to the light: they swarm candles, lanterns and torches until they smother them, shedding a fine grey dust that chokes whoever breathes it. A shrine full of them sounds like soft rain.

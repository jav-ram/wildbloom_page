A cluster of giant bubbles, each as wide as a person, shimmering with soapy colors and drifting gently in the damp air of the galleries. They are almost impossible to tell from ordinary bubbles, except for their size, the way they cluster, and the way they hold their shape far too well. Each bubble is filled with acid. The ooze attacks by slamming its bubbles into its prey, and by letting a few drift loose to burst.

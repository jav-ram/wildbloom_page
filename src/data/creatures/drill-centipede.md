A centipede 2 to 3 meters long, plated in dull grey chitin. Its head is a spiraling drill that actually turns, screwing it down through earth and soft stone. It waits underground, feeling for footsteps, then bursts up beneath its prey. Some hunt alone; others hunt in packs, and the ground around them is riddled with holes.

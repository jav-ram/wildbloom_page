A flyer so thin it is almost flat, grey and leathery, with a hooked, beakless mouth. At rest a flock hangs from the cliff edges in rows, swaying in the wind like old cloth drying, and climbers pass right beneath them. They attack anything clinging to the wall, trying to knock it loose and feed on what falls.

A kneeling Keeper of dark stone, head bowed, hands pressed together, dust on its shoulders: identical to the thousands of real ones around it. It is not stone. It is a creature that has taken their shape, and it waits among them in perfect stillness. Silence keeps it asleep. Sound wakes it, and the grinding of its first movement wakes the ones beside it.

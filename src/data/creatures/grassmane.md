A huge, gorilla-like brute that walks on four limbs, its front arms far longer and heavier than its legs. Its face is a lion's, framed by a long mane, and its whole body is covered in long, shaggy hair of the same pale gold as the grass of the Resting Place. It lies flat in the grass, completely still, until prey is close enough to touch. Then it rises roaring, and its fists do the rest.

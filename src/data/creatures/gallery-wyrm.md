A pale, eyeless serpent as long as a ship, its skin crusted with plates of stone it never sheds. It tunnels through the soft rock behind the wall, and many of the galleries are its old paths. Delvers feel it before they see it: a tremor in the floor, dust falling from the ceiling. When it surfaces, it brings the tunnel down with it.

A stone ox built by the Keepers, larger than any living ox, lying in a shrine like a carved statue. Its joints are sealed with seams of gold alloy that glow faintly when it moves. It is adorned as a shrine itself: bowls of spoiled food, burnt-down candles and incense lie at its feet. It has guarded its shrine since the Keepers left, and it rises when anyone disturbs it or takes an offering. A successful DC 14 Intelligence (Nature) check reveals it is no creature of nature, but something made.

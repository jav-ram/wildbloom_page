A thick carpet of green-gold moss, soft and warm to the touch, growing in the most sheltered corners of the terraces. It looks like the best bed a tired delver has seen in days, and it is: its warmth lulls anyone who lies on it into a deep sleep. Then it slowly closes over them and feeds.

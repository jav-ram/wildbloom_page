A fish about a meter long from mouth to tail that swims through the air, hovering no higher than a meter above the ground. It is covered in huge, bright-red scales layered like lacquered samurai armor, and its mouth is full of sharp teeth. Packs of them skim the tops of the grass in silence, then close in from every side at once. Delvers call them land barracudas.

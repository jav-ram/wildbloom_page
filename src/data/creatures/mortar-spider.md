A pale spider the size of a pony, with a swollen, chalky abdomen. Instead of silk, it spits a grey paste that sets like mortar within moments, gluing its prey to the gallery walls to be eaten later. Old victims stay where they were cemented, crusted over with layers of grey, and from a distance they look like carvings. Delvers who know better check every carving in the galleries twice.
